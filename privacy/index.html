Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Velari is a TV player for the IPTV lists and accounts you already have. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. What you set up stays on your TV.

What stays on your TV

Velari stores on the device, and only there:

  • the sources you add: the address of an M3U list or of a programme guide, and the server, username and password of an Xtream Codes account;
  • the catalogue and the guide downloaded from those sources;
  • what each profile keeps: favourites, My list, recently watched channels and titles, resume positions, visible categories and player settings;
  • the parental control PIN, stored only as a salted hash, and the locked categories.

The developer never receives any of this. It is not included in Android backups or device transfers: if you move to another TV, you add your sources again.

What leaves your TV, and where it goes

  • Your provider. To download the catalogue and play a channel or a title, Velari connects directly to the server you entered, sending the details it needs (for an Xtream account, your username and password). That connection is between your TV and your provider; it does not go through the developer. How your provider handles that information is governed by its own terms.
  • Images. Channel logos and posters are downloaded from the addresses given by your list or provider. Those requests identify the app as “Velari” with its version number.
  • Setting up from your phone. When you scan the QR code, your phone sends the details you type straight to your TV over your home network. They do not go to the internet.
  • These pages. If you open the privacy policy or the terms in the app, they are loaded from GitHub Pages, which, like any website, receives your IP address.

Crash reports

Velari does not include analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Android may send Google information about crashes and freezes of the app, and Google shows the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics in Google Play Console. That sharing is controlled by your device settings and Google’s privacy policy.

Children

Velari is not directed at children. Kids profiles and parental control are tools for adults to decide what can be watched at home; they do not collect any information.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase all data removes everything Velari has stored, and uninstalling the app does the same. As the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing else to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If Velari ever starts collecting information, the policy will say so before the app does.
