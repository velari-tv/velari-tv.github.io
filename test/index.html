Help us test Velari

Velari is an app for Android TV and Google TV to watch live TV channels, films and series from an IPTV list. Before it reaches everyone, it is in a private test. Thank you for helping!

1. Join the test

Use the same Google account you use on your TV for both steps.

  1. Join the Google Group velari-testers: open the link and press Join group.
  2. Open the test link and press Become a tester.
    Open the test link Link: {{OPT_IN_URL}}

2. Install it on your TV

You need a TV or TV box with Google TV or Android TV and the Play Store (Chromecast with Google TV, Mi TV Stick, a Sony, TCL or Philips TV…). Amazon Fire TV does not work.

  • On the TV, open the Play Store, search for Velari and install it.
  • Or, from your phone or computer, open Velari on Google Play and choose your TV under “Install on more devices”.

If it does not show up yet, wait a few hours after joining: Google takes a while to let you in.

3. Something to watch

Velari does not include channels: it plays the IPTV list you give it. If you don’t have one, these lists are free and legal (public channels that broadcast on the internet):

  • Spain, by TDTChannels: https://www.tdtchannels.com/lists/tv.m3u8
  • Other countries, by Free-TV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Free-TV/IPTV/master/playlist.m3u8

When you open Velari it asks you to add your channels:

  • The easy way: choose From your phone, scan the code with your phone and paste the link there.
  • Or choose M3U playlist, type the link with the remote and press Connect.

You can add more lists later in Settings › IPTV sources.

4. Tell us how it goes

Use it as you normally would. We especially want to know:

  • if something fails, freezes or closes by itself;
  • if something is slow;
  • if something is hard to understand or to find.

Write to velari.app.tv@gmail.com (a photo of the screen helps a lot), or leave private feedback on the Velari page in the Play Store.

Please stay in the test for at least 14 days. Google needs 12 testers for 14 days in a row before the app can go public; if someone leaves, the count starts again.
